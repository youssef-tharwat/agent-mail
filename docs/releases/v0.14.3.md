# Agent Mail v0.14.3

An idle delivery check now gets its 180-second acknowledgment window when the
first dispatch attempt is reserved. Previously, preparing the check started its
timer even while the recipient was busy. A long turn could therefore leave a
live route permanently expired with zero attempts.

Waiting consumes neither an attempt nor the acknowledgment window. Status has
no deadline until the first attempt; the first attempt reserves its deadline and
budget atomically. Later attempts, status reads and worker restarts retain that
deadline. Actual dispatched checks remain bounded to three attempts, 60 seconds
apart, and still expire normally.

Already-expired checks with zero attempts recover through this rule when their
recipient is eligible. This preserves their existing nonce and all notification
and task/mail retry history; no broad operator retry reset is required. A check
with no dispatch still cannot be acknowledged. Current identity, route health
and explicit recipient acknowledgment remain necessary for verified delivery.

Regression coverage includes a long busy wait, an older zero-attempt record,
worker restart, acknowledgment rejection before dispatch, normal expiry after
dispatch, and a delayed check carried by a real notification without creating a
second turn or resolving the underlying mail. Schema remains 27.
